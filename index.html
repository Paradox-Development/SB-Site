Sherry Blackman’s work has appeared in The Christian Science Monitor, The Star-Ledger, and a range of literary journals. She is the author of four books, an ordained minister, and has served as a truck stop chaplain and as pastor of the Church of the Mountain in Delaware Water Gap, Pennsylvania.
